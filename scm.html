Project Overview

This project analyzes supply chain operations to evaluate delivery performance, identify inefficiencies, and improve overall logistics processes.

Business Problem

Delays in deliveries and inconsistent supplier performance can negatively impact customer satisfaction. This project aims to identify the main causes of delays and improve operational efficiency.

Dashboard

Key Insights

  • 76% of orders were delivered on time
  • 105 orders experienced delays
  • Supplier performance varies across vendors

Tools & Skills Used

Excel SQL Power BI Data Analysis Supply Chain Analytics

Business Impact

The analysis helps businesses identify inefficiencies in their supply chain, reduce delays, and improve delivery performance.

Recommendations

  • Improve coordination for delayed orders
  • Monitor supplier performance regularly
  • Optimize logistics planning to increase efficiency
